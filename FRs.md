## Functional Requirements
- **FR-1 (User Management):** The system shall allow students and admins to register and log in using their name, email address, and phone number.
- **FR-2 (Item Posting):** The system shall allow students to create, edit, and mark posts as resolved, capturing the title, description, item type (lost or found), and campus location.
- **FR-3 (Photo Attachments):** The system shall allow students to upload one or more photos with captions and associate them with a specific item post.
- **FR-4 (Moderation Records):** The system shall require an admin to review pending item posts and log an approval record containing the decision (approve/reject), review comments, and review timestamp.
- **FR-5 (Search and Filtering):** The system shall provide a dedicated search feature that allows users to search posts by keyword and filter by item type and campus location.
