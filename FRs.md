## Users
- FR-1: A student can register an account.
- FR-2: A registered user can log in and log out.

## Lost & Found Items
- FR-3: A student can post a lost or found item with a title, description, category, campus location, and date.
- FR-4: Anyone can view a list of posted items.
- FR-5: Anyone can search items by keyword and filter by category, campus location, and status (lost / found).
- FR-6: A student can edit or delete their own posts.
- FR-7: A student can mark their item as resolved (returned / claimed).

## Moderation
- FR-8: An administrator can view all posts, including ones pending review.
- FR-9: An administrator can approve or reject a post; only approved posts are publicly visible.
- FR-10: An administrator can delete any post.
